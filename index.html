Guji is a coffee zone in the far south of Ethiopia, in the Oromia region, where coffee grows high on smallholder farms among the forest. Its cups are known for being bright, floral and full of stone fruit. We roast it light, in small batches, so that is what you taste.

What a month of Ember & Ore looks like

No warehouse shelf in between. Your bag is roasted for your delivery, not pulled from stock.

  1. Roast day

    Your share of the Guji goes into the roaster in a small batch and comes out light, sweet and loud with fruit.

  2. A day later

    Rested, bagged in a valve pouch, stamped with its roast date, sent out from Portland.

  3. At your door

    Peak flavour lands about a week to a month after roasting, which is exactly how long a 250 g bag lasts most people.

  4. The next one

    As the bag runs low, the next roast is already booked. Skip a month or cancel from the email, any time.

Brew it like we do

Light-roasted Guji likes hot water and a little patience. Our starting point for a pour-over, then adjust to taste.

Pour-over recipe
Coffee15 g, medium-fine
Water250 g at 94–96 °C
Bloom40 g, wait 40 seconds
PourThe rest in two slow pours
Total timeAbout 3 minutes
